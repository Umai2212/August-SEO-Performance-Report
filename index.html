Root causes for CTR decline

Pulling the losses together into a ranked diagnosis, before turning to what's still working.

Ranked causes

  • 1. AI Overviews (confirmed, partial) — 122 of ~206 lost clicks directly tied to 8 specific pages
  • 2. Position mix-shift — top-3 impression share fell from 12.3% to 4.7%; most new ranking activity landed in the lower-converting 4–10 range instead
  • 3. Device mix shift — mobile impression share grew (69.4% → 72.8%) while mobile CTR softened (1.32% → 1.09%)
  • 4. SERP crowding / Local Pack absence — confirmed on one query ("cet coaching bangalore"), not yet systematically checked across others
Ruled out: CTR does not cause position loss. Google has repeatedly denied CTR as a direct ranking factor. The pages that lost both clicks and position need a separate explanation, not attribution to falling CTR.
